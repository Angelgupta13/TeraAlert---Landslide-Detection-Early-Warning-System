Himalayan Landslide Detection Is Critical

The Challenge We Face

The Himalayan region faces some of the highest landslide risks globally. Every year, thousands of lives are affected by landslides triggered by heavy monsoon rains, earthquakes, and climate change. Early detection and warning systems can save countless lives.
